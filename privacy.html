GhostLab Family Privacy Policy

This policy covers the GhostLab family of apps from ADK Cyber (Jack Miller). It is not limited to GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit.

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Who we are

GhostLab apps are published by ADK Cyber / Jack Miller. Support is jmiller@adkcyber.com.

This family currently includes GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit, GhostLab: Tarot, GhostLab: Eight Ball, GhostLab: Palmistry, and GhostLab: Night Glide.

Family practices

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • When an app offers purchases, those purchases go through Apple StoreKit on the App Store.
  • You can email support at jmiller@adkcyber.com with privacy questions about any GhostLab app.

Each app below is described only from what that product actually uses. A statement that is true for one title (such as “no ads” for Paranormal Toolkit) is not a family-wide claim.

GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit

GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit (formerly SpectralEVP) has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no servers of our own. Investigation recordings, videos, notes, and settings stay on your device unless you export or share them.

The app may ask iOS for:

  • Microphone for EVP sessions and Spirit Speak Live Chat. Recordings stay in the on-device Library.
  • Speech recognition to loosely match spoken questions. Depending on device settings, Apple may process speech. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not store that audio off-device.
  • Camera for the low-light overlay, Motion-Grid, SLS view, and optional session video. Video is saved with the investigation on this device.
  • Photos (add only) when you tap “Save to Photos.” The app cannot read your existing photos.
  • Location (while using the app) for nearby places, compass heading, and tagging where a session was recorded. Location stays in the session files on your device.
  • Barometer to log environmental pressure trends during sessions.
  • Local network (Team) for peer-to-peer sharing between nearby phones. Nothing passes through our servers.
  • Ultra-wideband (Nearby Interaction) to measure distance to a second U1-capable device used as a trigger object.
  • Apple Watch companion commands via WatchConnectivity, device to device.

You can decline or revoke any permission in iOS Settings. The related feature will not function.

GhostLab: Tarot

GhostLab: Tarot (App Store bundle com.cloudpet.app) is an on-device card lab. It is entertainment only. It does not provide user accounts.

Readings and journal entries stay on your device unless you share them.

The app’s PrivacyInfo file declares NSPrivacyTracking false, lists no collected data types, and reports on-device UserDefaults access (reason CA92.1).

  • Microphone so you can speak a question or statement. We do not store that audio.
  • Apple speech recognition to turn a spoken question into text. Apple may process speech. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The current Info.plist has no camera usage string.
